About

A tradesman who takes pride
in getting it right

Jonny Lea has been fitting kitchens and carrying out property improvements across West Sussex for over two decades — building a reputation on honest communication, meticulous workmanship, and the ability to solve problems as they arise.

JP Lea Installations is a sole trader operation built on repeat business and word-of-mouth referrals. When Jonny takes on a project, it gets his full attention — no juggling multiple jobs, no cutting corners. He works cleanly and efficiently, keeping disruption to a minimum throughout.

With multi-trade skills spanning carpentry, tiling, plastering, painting and decorating, Jonny can handle all aspects of a kitchen project himself, and works only with long-standing, trusted, qualified electricians and plumbers where specialist trades are required.

Whether you have a kitchen ready to be fitted or want help designing the whole space from scratch, Jonny can guide you through every step — including spotting improvements to your supplier's plan you might never have noticed yourself.

  • Installation only, or full design, supply and install
  • All worktop materials: granite, quartz, solid wood, laminate, acrylic, marble
  • Plastering, tiling, painting & decorating all in-house
  • Electrical and plumbing via long-trusted qualified tradespeople
  • Domestic, commercial and insurance work all undertaken
  • Free estimates, no obligation
